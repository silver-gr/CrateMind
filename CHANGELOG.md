# Changelog

All notable changes to CrateMind are documented here.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/). Versioning is [Semantic](https://semver.org/).
CrateMind stays at 0.x while the desktop app is a preview.

---

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-28 — Clean artist and title names, a new deck layout with a top bar, transitions timed from the track's structure, build-ups and drops, a master safety limiter and right-click menus

With «Καθαρά ονόματα» on, CrateMind shows one clean spelling of every artist and title, built from your files' own tags and your answers, says why each name reads as it does, and asks you on a new «Καθαρισμός ονομάτων» page where it is not sure; the «Καλλιτέχνες» page then groups tracks by those artists. Nothing is written to your files, and turning the switch off brings every name and the old artist list back as they were. Two-deck mode gets a new layout: a top bar on every page with the master level meter, «REC», the analysis progress and the theme and background buttons; a facts row under the waveforms that counts the bars to the next part; a narrower mixer with one knob column per deck and larger knobs that show their value; and, on a wide window, a dock that sits under the page while the sidebar and the side panels run to the bottom of the window. Automatic transitions can be timed from each track's own structure, which now tells build-ups from drops, and four hand-picked transitions join the list. The master output gets an always-on safety limiter, the LOW knob can remove kick and bass completely, and Auto Gain no longer silences tracks with damaged peaks. Sync handles half and double tempo, «Q» quantizes loops and cues, empty pads can suggest cues, and on the controller the FX buttons and Shift + BEATS can pick an effect for one deck, the one that played last, while Wet/Dry and BEATS now follow that same deck. Right-click menus reach every track, set, crate, deck and cue pad, and the decks can rate energy and darkness with one click. The Library toolbar is rebuilt in two rows, and the Library comes back at once when you return to it. The search language gains `OR`, `sort:`, `limit:`, `plays:`, key lists and `no_energy`; sets can be renamed, deleted and read as a story; a running analysis can be stopped; a scan no longer marks half a folder missing at once; and moved tracks are relinked on your word. Artist research's AI model is now set up in «Ρυθμίσεις», works with more models and can research every artist again, and a plain-Greek «Λεξικό όρων» explains the app's terms.

> **After updating:** the first launch backs up the library, then adds a few columns and tables to it: how sure CrateMind is of a grid's bar line, each file's tags as they were read, your answers about names, the clean names, and one empty table that a later version will use. Nothing in this version writes to your audio files. A library-exchange file from this version is refused by older installs. Then, in this order:
>
> 1. **Run one scan** in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Σάρωση» («Έλεγχος αλλαγών» is enough). It reads every file's tags once, read only, and keeps them for «Καθαρά ονόματα»; on a large library it takes a few minutes. Until it has run, «Καθαρά ονόματα» cannot be turned on, and its section in «Ρυθμίσεις» says how many tracks are not read yet.
> 2. **Run «BPM + Key · μόνο νέα» once** in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «BPM & Key». It reads every track's structure again with version 4, which finds build-ups and drops, and it checks each MP3's header once, so the few MP3s whose start the deck and the analysis read differently get their beat grid and structure measured again (see Fixed). It opens every track's audio once, so on a large library it runs for a long while; «Διακοπή» keeps the tracks it has finished, and the next «μόνο νέα» goes on with the rest. BPM, keys and loudness are not analysed again. Until that run, no track shows «Build-up» or «Drop». The «?» on an uncertain bar line needs a grid measured by this version (a new track, or one of those MP3s), so most tracks will not show it yet.
> 3. **When you want, turn on «Καθαρά ονόματα»** in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Καθαρά ονόματα». A names update then runs in the background and takes seconds (it waits until a running scan, analysis or other task ends, and while a two-deck set plays); after it, the clean names show and the «Καλλιτέχνες» page lists artists by them. An artist whose tracks were split up or merged with another's may start without a research finding: answer the questions on «Καθαρισμός ονομάτων» before you run «Νέα έρευνα για όλους», so the research is not spent on names that will still change. Turn the switch off and every name, every search and the old artist list are as before.
>
> Memory Crates (saved searches) that use a negated filter (`-status:retired`, `-energy:9`, `-role:peak` and the like) may now list more tracks: tracks without a rating, or with that field empty, now count as "not X" (see Fixed).
>
> Every new switch starts off: «Χρονισμός από τη δομή · για όλες τις μεταβάσεις» (transition editor), «Q» (mixer), «Καθαρά ονόματα», «Προτεινόμενα cues από τη δομή», «Γρήγορη βαθμολογία στα decks» and «Η κυματομορφή ακολουθεί το EQ» (in «Ρυθμίσεις»), and «Τα κουμπιά FX διαλέγουν εφέ», «Jog nudge όταν παίζει το deck» and «Καταγραφή όλων των μηνυμάτων» (the controller card in «Ρυθμίσεις»; the log is off at every start). With them off, names, loops, cues, the waveforms, the jog wheel, the FX buttons and transitions work as before.
>
> **The look changes on its own:** the default background is now «Balatro» (a background still on the old default, «Soft Aurora», moves to it once; choose «Soft Aurora» again from the top bar or «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Εμφάνιση» to keep it), the «CrateMind» waveform colours are now a palette of their own (the old default looked almost the same as djay's; «djay (συχνότητες)» in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Κυματομορφές» gives you those colours), the sidebar starts at its new, narrower width, and a two-deck dock left at the old default height moves to the new one. If you had set up artist research before, its connection moves to «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Μοντέλο AI» by itself.

### Added

#### Decks and mixer
- **A top bar on every page** — the window's title strip now carries the controller status (desktop app), a chip with the progress of a running analysis («· παύση» while the decks hold it back), «Ένταση ακρόασης» with the master level meter on the window's centre line, «REC» to its right, the theme and background buttons, and a clock. Every empty stretch still drags the window. On a narrower window the background button, then the theme button, then the clock drop out; both choices stay in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Εμφάνιση» and in the ⌘K palette's «Εμφάνιση» group.
- **A safety limiter on the master** — two beatmatched kicks can add up to 6 dB, so blends could clip at the speakers and in recordings. The master output now always passes through a limiter with a -1 dB ceiling before the speakers and «REC». It steps aside while the Maximizer is in use. A single track played in the Library stays off the master as before.
- **A master level meter** — left and right peak bars for what goes to the speakers and the recording, with a one-second peak hold and a «LIM» light while the safety limiter is working. With one deck playing outside the master, it says «εκτός master», and «REC» refuses to start instead of recording silence, saying how to fix it (open the second deck, or turn on Auto Gain or the Limiter and load the track again). With no track loaded, «REC» starts with one deck too.
- **A facts row under the waveforms** — in two-deck mode each deck's half shows its title, the time left (rose in the last 30 seconds), the tempo, the pitch, the key, the next loop length and the bars to the next part («16 bars → Break», amber from 8 bars), and the centre shows whether the two keys fit and the tempo gap («Δ 1.1 BPM»). Each half is framed in its deck's colour, more softly at the row's outer ends and along its bottom. The seek overview moved into this row: hovering it shows how far a point is from the playhead in bars («+16 bars», «≈» on an estimated grid) and, on a wide overview, the part's name; a click still seeks as before.
- **Quantize** — a «Q» in the mixer (off by default) makes loops and hot cues land on the beat: new cues are saved on the nearest beat, loops start on a beat and last whole beats, and a cue jump on a playing deck keeps the beat phase. It acts only on a grid Sync would trust; with no such grid, or with «Q» off, everything is as before.
- **Suggested cues from the structure** — with «Προτεινόμενα cues από τη δομή» on in «Ρυθμίσεις», empty pads 5 to 8 show faint suggestions: «Πρώτο μπάσο» (the first drop, not the build-up before it), «Break», «Πριν το drop» and «Τέλος μπάσου», each on a bar line. A press jumps there and saves nothing; «Κράτα ως cue» keeps it as an ordinary cue. The controller's pad lights stay dark for suggestions, and «Phrase Finder» marks the points with their pad number.
- **An uncertain bar line shows «?»** — when CrateMind is not sure which beat is the 1 of its own grid, the bar numbers in the deck's waveform read «12?» and the downbeat tool reads «DOWNBEAT ?», with a tooltip on how to set it. A downbeat you set, a djay grid or an estimated grid never shows it.
- **Headphone knobs on screen** — the controller's headphone gain and mix knobs move «CUE GAIN» and «CUE MIX» at the top of the mixer. There is no headphone output yet, so they change no sound; they are dimmed and say so.
- **The deck waveforms can follow the EQ** — with «Η κυματομορφή ακολουθεί το EQ» on in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Κυματομορφές» (off by default), turning a deck's LOW, MID or HIGH down fades that band's colour out of the two big scrolling waveforms and makes the waveform fainter there; its shape never changes. During an automatic transition they follow the transition's own EQ. Nothing is analysed again.
- **«Έλεγχος εξόδου ακουστικών» (macOS desktop app)** — a one-time check in «Ρυθμίσεις» › «Αναπαραγωγή» that plays a beep on outputs 3-4 of your sound card and logs what the system reports, so a later version can send the cue to your headphones. It puts every setting back when it stops and stops by itself after ten minutes.

#### Transitions
- **Transitions timed from the track's structure** — with «Χρονισμός από τη δομή · για όλες τις μεταβάσεις» on in the transition editor, a transition without a saved window puts the bass swap on the outgoing track's last phrase line before its bass stops, and starts the incoming track so that its first drop lands on the swap. Saved windows and entry/exit cues still win, and each side falls back to the old rule when it has no usable structure. On a copy of a real library this removed the stretch with no bass from either track in almost every pair (the median gap went from 25 bars to 0). The editor draws the phrase lines and both bass points.
- **A tempo stretch chip** — pairs show «Stretch N %» above 8% with tempo match on, and «Χωρίς ταίριασμα tempo» above 16%.
- **Transition warnings** — the editor flags, and never changes, three things: «Δύο μπάσα μαζί» (both basses heard together for more than 4 bars; a build-up counts as bass), «Εκτός φράσης» (the two tracks' phrase lines never meet inside the window) and «Απότομη αλλαγή τονικότητας» (keys that do not fit on the Camelot wheel heard together for more than 8 bars). Each needs sure data and stays quiet without it. The set list shows the key warning.
- **Four hand-picked transitions** — «Έξοδος με echo» (16 bars: the outgoing track cuts in the middle while its Echo rings on), «Μακριά ανάμειξη» (32 bars: the incoming track enters without lows, the basses swap halfway, the outgoing mids and highs leave over the last quarter), «Ηρεμία και drop» (16 bars: the outgoing track thins out and drops to a short lull, the incoming one lands at full weight on the window's end) and «Μία ζώνη τη φορά» (16 bars: highs, then mids, then the lows at the end, so the two basses are never heard together). You choose them in the editor; «Αυτόματο» never picks them. Without the effects rack, «Έξοδος με echo» plays as a plain cut and says so, and switching its echo off by hand puts the deck's own effect back.
- **Reuse a transition you tuned in another set** — when another set holds a saved transition for the same two tracks in the same order, the pair shows «Ρυθμισμένη στο set «…»» with «Χρήση», which copies it into this set as its own. Nothing is copied by itself.

#### Sets
- **Rename, delete and manage sets** — «Μετονομασία…» renames a set. «Διαχείριση sets…» in the Set Builder's «…» lists every set by name, date or size, with a filter, so you can rename one or tick several and delete them. «Διαγραφή set…» asks first and names the set and its track count. Tracks, ratings and pair notes stay, plays stay in the history, and each deleted set keeps its own entry in the audit log.
- **«Προσθήκη σε άλλο set»** — each set row can go to another set, with the sets you used this session first and «Νέο set…» to start one with the track.
- **«Η αφήγηση του set»** — a panel in the Set Builder, above «Καμπύλη ενέργειας», reads an ordered set as chapters of steady tempo, names how each chapter begins (a blend, a step, a jump, a 3:4 change), sets single-track detours aside and says when the tempo, loudness or bass drift inside a long chapter. It only reads; nothing is stored.

#### Library, search and tagging
- **Right-click menus everywhere** — tracks in the Library, its history tab, the Set Builder, Flow Finder, the dashboard's «Πρόσφατα», «Ιστορικό», «Χαρακτηρισμός tracks» and the duplicate groups have a menu: play, load to Deck A or B, add to a set, edit, status, BPM «×½» / «×2», BPM analysis, Flow Finder, the artist's tracks, show in folder, copy. Sets, crates, history sessions, duplicate groups, moved-track pairs, artists, the Library's column headers and the Set Builder's transitions have their own menus. The decks have one too («Αφαίρεση από το Deck», «BPM» with «×½» / «×2», and the track's items), and the cue pads get «Ανάθεση αποθηκευμένου cue», «Άδειασμα pad (το cue διατηρείται)», «Διαγραφή cue…» (asks first) and «Ρυθμίσεις pad…». Every item does what the matching button does, and a right-click never seeks or fires a pad. A touch or pen long-press opens the menu too.
- **Fast rating on the decks** — with «Γρήγορη βαθμολογία στα decks» on in «Ρυθμίσεις», each deck in two-deck mode gets a status menu and «Ενέργεια» and «Σκοτεινότητα» bars in its info column (the single deck shows the two bars under its title). One click writes the value under the pointer, and the message shows «ενέργεια 6 → 8» with «Αναίρεση». In the Set Builder the energy column is clickable the same way. In «Χαρακτηρισμός tracks», Option + digit (Alt on Windows) writes energy 1-10 while the plain digits still jump to cues, and a new queue «Παιγμένα χωρίς ενέργεια» lists the tracks you played but never rated. A deck holding a track always shows its latest status, energy and darkness, whether you changed them in the editor, a menu or a bulk edit.
- **More in the search language** — `OR` between terms (`psytrance OR goa`), `plays:` for play counts, key lists (`key:8A,9A`), `no_energy` for tracks without an energy, `sort:bpm` / `sort:-energy` and `limit:50`. `limit:` applies everywhere the search is used: counts, crates, facets and bulk edit. Details in `docs/README.md#search-syntax`.
- **Library helpers** — «Ταιριάζει με το Deck A/B» chips (while a deck holds a track) search for a tempo within about 6% and the keys that fit; the ⌘K palette finds tracks after two letters and offers «Φόρτωση στο Deck A/B»; tracks you played since the app started get a small dot; the status line shows the track count and total length of the list.
- **Moved tracks, relinked on your word** — a file that was moved and retagged used to leave its old row missing, with your ratings on it. The Duplicates page now lists such pairs under «Μετακινημένα κομμάτια» (same audio fingerprint, length within 3 seconds, one candidate on each side). Only a pair you confirm is merged: your work moves to the new row, the retagged file keeps its new tags, and its source links and hand-corrected names come along. No file is read, written or moved. Each duplicate group also suggests which copy to keep, with the reason (lossless, higher bitrate, your ratings or plays, longer).
- **«Αυτόματες συνδέσεις» in «Ενοποιημένη βιβλιοθήκη»** — rules that join a local file to its Spotify or SoundCloud record, and count duplicate remote records as one recording, when names match closely and lengths agree within 2 seconds. The app never runs them by itself: they are applied only by a reviewed run with the app closed and a verified backup. The new tab lists what such a run linked, by batch, with an undo for each row. Your own rejections and hand-made links are never overridden. Only CrateMind's own data is written.
- **«Μοντέλο AI» in «Ρυθμίσεις»** — artist research's AI connection (address, model, effort and API key) is now set up in the app, with «Δοκιμή σύνδεσης» to try the form before saving. Once stored, the key is never shown again: the page shows only its last four characters, with «Αλλαγή» and «Διαγραφή». It is kept in a file for your user account on this computer, not encrypted (the page says who can read it), and never in a backup, an exchange file or an export. A value set by a `CRATEMIND_LLM1_*` variable wins and the page names it.
- **«Νέα έρευνα για όλους» on the «Καλλιτέχνες» page** — researches again, with the model you set up now, every artist with two or more tracks that has no finding from that model yet (another model's, none, or a failed one), three at a time and with no cap of 100. The dialog says how many artists, which model and roughly how long. Genres you confirmed are never touched, and each current finding stays as it is until its new one arrives and passes the source check; a failed call leaves it unchanged. «Διακοπή» stops any research run: no new call starts, and the calls already out finish and are kept. Pressing the button again goes on with the artists that are left.
- **Research runs stop when the provider keeps failing** — any artist research run now stops by itself when its first 3 answers all fail, or 5 in a row fail for the same reason, and says why. An answer that fails the source check does not count toward the five: it says more about the artist than about the provider. Failures now have Greek reasons («Ο πάροχος απέρριψε το κλειδί API», a usage or billing limit, an error at the provider, no connection to the provider, an answer that failed the source check), and the end message names the commonest one.
- **«Λεξικό όρων»** — a plain-Greek glossary of 27 terms (dB, LUFS, headroom, the limiters, «εκτός master», EQ and the LOW cut, BPM, beat grid and downbeat, phrase (with build-up and drop), Camelot, Sync, Quantize, cues, tempo stretch, clean names and more): what each is, what you hear or see, and which control to use. It has a search and, on a wide window, a contents column beside the terms; it opens from ⌘K, «Ρυθμίσεις» and «Τι σημαίνει;» links in «Ένταση ακρόασης», «Key & Harmony» and «Phrase Finder».
- **«Σχετικά με το CrateMind» as its own window** — on both systems and in the web version: the logo on a turning record, version, the data-safety promise, four numbers from your library, the credits, the licence and links. It opens from the Mac menu, ⌘K and «Ρυθμίσεις».

#### Clean names
- **«Καθαρά ονόματα»** — a switch in «Ρυθμίσεις» (off by default). With it on, CrateMind shows a clean artist and title for every track, worked out from the file's own tags, its file and folder names, its Spotify or SoundCloud links and your answers: the same spelling for the same artist across the library, no BPM, «FULL TRACK» or other junk in a title, and the artist and the title each in its place. The Library, the track editor, «Χαρακτηρισμός tracks», the Set Builder, «Ιστορικό», Flow Finder and the Duplicates page show those names; BPM is the same on and off. Each name has a state: «Για ματιά» (it rests on one clue), «Για απόφαση» (the clues disagree and it needs your answer), «Χωρίς καλλιτέχνη» (shown as «Άγνωστος καλλιτέχνης»), «Δική σου διόρθωση», and no mark for a sure one. On a copy of a real library about 1,800 of 4,675 tracks read differently. While the switch is on, the names update by themselves after a scan, an analysis or an answer, in seconds, and «Ενημέρωση ονομάτων» updates them at once; Settings shows how many names are in each state, how many tracks are not read yet and «Άλλαξαν από την τελευταία ματιά: N». A track outside your music folders (one you played from Downloads, say) never blocks the switch and keeps its usual name. Turn it off and every name and every search is exactly as before. It only changes what the app shows: your files, their tags and the stored titles stay as they are.
- **«Γιατί αυτό το όνομα»** — in a track's «Στοιχεία αρχείου», a block says why the track has its name: its state, one Greek sentence per rule with the rule's number («κανόνας N7»), the open question if there is one, the facts it found (tempo, original artist, label, remixers), the folder name, the tags as the file holds them and the name with «Καθαρά ονόματα» off. «Διόρθωση» lets you type the name yourself, «Εντάξει» says a «Για ματιά» name is fine, and «Αναίρεση» takes your correction back.
- **The «Καθαρισμός ονομάτων» page** — a new page in the sidebar and ⌘K where you answer and check names. «Για απόφαση» lists the questions with their tracks and lettered answers («Άλλο…» to type your own); «Ερωτήσεις προτιμήσεων» asks how you like a few patterns written; «Για ματιά» groups those tracks by the rule that named them, with «Εντάξει» for a whole group (it says how many tracks first) and «Διόρθωση» for one track; «Χωρίς καλλιτέχνη» lets you type an artist; «Άλλαξαν» lists names that a later update changed, with «Εντάξει» or «Κράτα το προηγούμενο». «Οι απαντήσεις σου» lists every answer with how many tracks it changed («άλλαξε N κομμάτια») and «Αναίρεση». An answer about an artist applies by name, so a track you add later with that artist follows it.
- **Search finds both spellings** — while «Καθαρά ονόματα» is on, a search finds a track by its clean name and by the spelling in its tags, and `artist:` matches both. New `names:` filters (`names:look`, `names:open`, `names:none`, `names:hand`, `names:sure`, `names:changed` for a clean name that differs from the one shown with the switch off, `names:new` for a change you have not looked at), with quick filters for them in the Library while the switch is on.
- **Your answers travel with the library** — answers and corrections are kept in the backups, in exchange files (as «Αποφάσεις ονομάτων») and in the JSON export. A library brought in from an exchange file starts with an empty «Άλλαξαν» list.
- **Not yet on the clean names** — exports (M3U8, CSV, Markdown) keep the stored names, and so do the order of `limit:` and of «Χαρακτηρισμός tracks», «Μετακινημένα κομμάτια» and the pages under «Ενοποιημένη βιβλιοθήκη». A track already on a deck keeps the name it loaded with until you load it again.
- **Artists by their clean names** — with «Καθαρά ονόματα» on, the «Καλλιτέχνες» page and the Library's «Καλλιτέχνες» tab list one artist per artist folder the cleaning chose (for a collaboration usually its first artist; a pair whose names are never seen alone stays a pair), so an artist written two ways is one artist. A track's genre from its artist, the `artist_*:` and `substyle:` searches and Flow Finder follow those artists. A track that waits for your answer, or has no artist, joins an artist once it has one. Each clean-name artist takes the research finding and the genre you confirmed from the old artist that gave it the most tracks, when more than half of that old artist's tracks went to it; the others (the smaller parts of a split, a merge of equal parts) start without one and can be researched again with «Νέα έρευνα για όλους». The switch and artist research wait for each other: the switch cannot be turned while research runs, and research and «Ανανέωση λίστας» wait while a names update runs. Turn the switch off and the old list comes back as it was, with its findings; turn it on again and the clean-name artists come back with theirs.
- **A one-time import of prepared answers** — `reconciliation/names_import.py` brings answers prepared outside the app into «Καθαρά ονόματα»: a dry run on a backup copy first, then the real run with its own verified backup and the app closed. The app never runs it.

#### Analysis
- **Stop a running analysis** — «Διακοπή» in «Ρυθμίσεις» stops a BPM/key analysis between tracks: tracks already analysed are kept, the rest are left as they were, and the task ends with «Διακόπηκε στο βήμα X από N» (the steps of every phase, about three per track in a full run). «Επανανάλυση» now asks first, in plain words: how many tracks, roughly how long, what it keeps (manual BPM, cues, sets, tags) and that the music drive must stay connected.

#### Controller
- **The FX buttons and Shift + BEATS pick effects** — with «Τα κουμπιά FX διαλέγουν εφέ» on (off by default; while off, the six FX buttons stay the transition-note buttons), each FX button sets its effect on one deck, the deck that played last or else the one whose effect was turned on last, and leaves the other deck alone (by default HPF Highpass, LPF Lowpass, FLANGER Flanger, ECHO Echo, REVERB Reverb, PHASER Phaser), and Shift + button stores that deck's current effect on the button. Shift + BEATS always steps that same one deck through the effect list, in the picker's order, stopping at the ends, and the deck's list opens while you hold Shift. A plain BEATS turn still changes the beat length, now of that deck only (see Changed).
- **The screen knob follows the controller** — turning EQ, TRIM, FILTER, CUE GAIN, CUE MIX or Wet/Dry on the controller shows that knob's value pill on screen until a second after the last move.
- **«Jog nudge όταν παίζει το deck»** — off by default. While a deck plays, turning the jog wheel bends its tempo in that direction, as the bend buttons do, and lets go 80 ms after the last tick. A paused deck, Shift + jog, a deck in a set mix and a held bend button keep the jog as it was. Not yet tried on the controller.
- **«Καταγραφή όλων των μηνυμάτων»** — a switch in the controller card of «Ρυθμίσεις» lists every message the controller sends, newest first, with what CrateMind made of it in Greek («Deck B · Sync», «EQ LOW», «Shift πατημένο», «χωρίς αντιστοίχιση»), to track down a knob that moves the wrong thing. It is kept in memory only and is off at every start.

#### Safety
- **A scan no longer marks half a folder missing at once** — a music folder with at least 20 known tracks that would lose more than half of them in one scan (an emptied folder, the wrong disk) now marks none missing and ends with a warning that names the folder and the numbers. A button in the scan card runs it again allowing it.

### Fixed
- **Auto Gain silenced tracks with damaged peaks** — some MP3s report impossible true peaks (+10 to +81 dBTP) from damaged spots, and Auto Gain turned them down by up to 82 dB; a few played silent. A peak above +6 dBTP is no longer believed, the gain stays within -24 to +12 dB, a track at or below the target is never turned down, and an impossible loudness gives no Auto Gain at all. Nothing needs measuring again.
- **A forced analysis could empty BPM and key** — when «Επανανάλυση» could not read a file (for example a music drive that dropped out), it wrote empty BPM and key over the stored values, and «μόνο νέα» never tried again. The last values are now kept.
- **Analysis stopped at a switched-off folder** — tracks under a music folder switched off in «Ρυθμίσεις» stopped the analysis with «Μη διαθέσιμη διαδρομή (disabled)», counted as loudness failures and left the progress short. They are now left as they are and counted as done; a drive that is not connected still stops the run, and a failed step drops its queued tracks instead of working through them.
- **MP3s whose beat grid sat slightly off** — for MP3s without an encoder header, the deck and the analysis disagreed on where the audio starts, so 24 tracks in one library sat 12 to 25 ms off their grid and their waveform peaks. The grid, the structure and the deck's scrolling waveform are now measured on the deck's own time zero. An MP3 with junk before its first audio frame, which the desktop app's player cannot decode, now gets a Greek explanation and a way out instead of a generic error.
- **Negated filters dropped untagged tracks** — `-retired`, `-status:retired`, `-energy:9`, `-role:peak` and the like left out every track without a profile, and tagged tracks whose field was empty. They now count as "not X", as `-bpm:` and `-key:` already did. `bpm:150-137` (whole numbers, high first) now works like `bpm:137-150`.
- **A rename that only changed letter case** — on the Mac's disk, renaming `Song.wav` to `Song.WAV` marked the track missing until the next scan. It is now followed in one scan.
- **Controller lights that stayed dark** — when the controller's input connected before its output was listed (after plugging it in), the lights and jog screens stayed dark until the next replug. CrateMind now keeps trying the output every 2 seconds.
- **«Εμφάνιση στον φάκελο» on Windows** — it opened Explorer's default folder; it now opens the track's folder with the file selected.
- **English and internal words on screen** — «Χαρακτηρισμός tracks» showed internal values in its messages («ανοχή huge», raw substyle keys) and English labels (Genre, Label, Format), and a scan that also carried a warning showed its analysis counts in English. All of it is Greek now.
- **Smaller fixes** — the history rows show a BPM changed with «×½» / «×2» right away; a transition without a bar count can no longer freeze the page; folding the sidebar while the window was in the background could leave it stuck at its old width; the sidebar logo's glow no longer ends in a hard square edge.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-24 — Free software under GPL-3.0, set recording, an effects rack on each deck, a play history and better keys

CrateMind is now free software under the GPL-3.0. The two-deck mixer can record your set to a WAV or AAC file, and each deck gets a built-in effects rack. CrateMind keeps a history of what you actually play, and after a crash it offers to bring the decks back. Keys are detected from the whole track with a trained method, djay's key is used too, and an uncertain key is marked «?». The Library gets a «LUFS» column, a «Δομή» column, «Ομαδοποίηση» and a tabbed side panel with artists and history. Suggestions come 12 per page with a musical match score, the duplicate check also compares the sound itself, and «Ρυθμίσεις» is now a sidebar of topics.

> **Licence:** from this release CrateMind is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 only (GPL-3.0-only). The licence text is in `LICENSE` in the repository, and the source code is on GitHub. Every download carries the licence texts of all the packages it ships and a `NOTICES.txt` that says where to get the source of each bundled media tool. Earlier versions are no longer offered for download. GSAP, whose licence stood in the way of an open licence, is gone: the page reveals and the dashboard spotlight now use the browser's own animations and look the same.

> **After updating:** the first launch adds columns for keys, loudness and play history to the library (with a backup first when the library lives in a dataset folder) and fills in djay's keys for tracks you imported from djay. Then run «BPM + Key · μόνο νέα» in «Ρυθμίσεις» once: it detects every track's key again with the new method and measures every track's loudness. BPM and beat grids are not analysed again. Some keys will change, because the order of key sources changed (see Changed). A library-exchange file from this version is refused by older installs.

### Added
- **Record a set** — a «REC» button in the two-deck mixer records what goes to the speakers, as a 24-bit WAV in your export folder. A «REC 12:34» pill stays at the top of the window on every page, and stopping takes two presses («Σίγουρα;»), so a stray click never ends a set. The file stays valid if the app crashes mid-set. «Μορφή εγγραφής set» in «Ρυθμίσεις» chooses WAV (default), AAC 256 kbps or AAC 128 kbps: in AAC mode the WAV is converted when you stop and deleted only after the AAC file checks out; if the conversion fails, the WAV stays. Play counts and «last played» now reflect real use, so `not_recent:` and the suggestions' «played recently» do too.

If the app closes unexpectedly, the next start (within 24 hours) asks «Επαναφορά των decks από την προηγούμενη συνεδρία;» and names the tracks. «Επαναφορά» loads them paused at the same position, with tempo, key lock, loop and mixer settings; nothing starts playing by itself. The deck's small seek overview gets ticks where the bass comes in and stops; click one to jump there. The scrolling waveform labels each part («Break · 16 bars»).

### Added

The editors show «Διέγερση», «Συναισθηματικό σθένος» and your vocabularies instead of the DJ fields. The digit keys set «Διέγερση» and Shift+digit sets «Συναισθηματικό σθένος», also while a deck is loaded.
- **«Συναισθηματικό σθένος»** — a new 1–10 scale for how positive or negative a track feels. Together with «Διέγερση» it forms the two axes of the circumplex model used in music-emotion research. Search it with `valence:6-8`; the CSV export includes it.
- **Vocabularies** — build your own tag vocabularies in Ρυθμίσεις > «Λεξιλόγια», such as «Θεραπευτικός στόχος» or «Διάθεση», each allowing one value or several per track. «Φόρτωση προτεινόμενου λεξιλογίου (μουσικοθεραπεία)» adds a draft of 8 vocabularies with 47 values that you can edit freely. You pick values as chips in «Ουρά tagging» and the Library editor, Tag Cloud groups them under their vocabulary, and the JSON backup saves them. Search with tokens such as `purpose:χαλάρωση`, or `-purpose:*` for tracks with no value yet.
- **Bulk tagging** — tick tracks in the Library (Shift-click selects a range) or take all search results, then use «Προσθήκη τιμών» or «Αφαίρεση τιμών» on «Επιλεγμένα (N)» or «Όλα τα αποτελέσματα (N)». Before anything is written, the confirmation shows how many tracks will change and how many single-value tags will be replaced. The whole change is applied in one step, and each track's change is logged.
- **Sorting help in «Ουρά tagging»** — a «Χωρίς …» queue for each vocabulary, «Από φάκελο…» with a folder list and track counts, and a «Ταξινομημένα x / y» progress line. Queues load 500 tracks at a time, so large libraries open quickly.
- **Folder search** — `folder:` followed by part of a path, written with `\` or `/`, finds the tracks under matching folders.
- **BPM and key analysis in the desktop app** — the Windows and macOS apps now analyse BPM and key themselves, with the same librosa analysis as the web version, so both give the same values on a shared library. New tracks are analysed automatically after a scan, and «BPM + Key · Μόνο νέα» and «Επανανάλυση» appear in Ρυθμίσεις. Analysis runs in a separate, lower-priority process so playback stays smooth, and it stops cleanly when you quit. Essentia and the ReccoBeats, GetSongBPM and Cyanite comparisons, including the BPM review card, stay in the web version. The Windows installer grows by about 50 MB.

### Changed
- **Search ignores accents and matches partial words** — «χαλαρωση» finds «Χαλάρωση», «μανος» finds «Μάνος» and a final ς matches σ, also in field searches such as `artist:μάνος`. A word you are still typing matches every word it starts. Apostrophes no longer break a search («Σ' αγαπώ»); only double quotes group words. The first launch after the update rebuilds the search index once, right after the launch backup, which adds a few seconds on a large library.
- **CSV export** — adds a «Συναισθηματικό σθένος» column and one column per vocabulary, and starts with a UTF-8 BOM so Excel shows Greek text correctly.
- **Greek analysis messages** — a missing ffmpeg, a busy analysis and an interrupted analysis now report in Greek («Δεν βρέθηκε το ffmpeg…», «Εκτελείται ήδη άλλη εργασία…», «Η ανάλυση BPM/key σταμάτησε πριν ολοκληρωθεί…»).

### Fixed
- **«Όλα» in «Ουρά tagging»** — the «Όλα» queue showed no tracks; it now lists the whole library, 500 tracks at a time.
- **Searches in very large libraries** — a search word, or an excluded word, that matched more than 32,766 tracks made the search fail. Search, listing and bulk tagging now work at any library size.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-01 — The first installable desktop preview: two decks, a controller, djay beatgrids and a Windows installer

CrateMind becomes a desktop app with its own two-deck player, a Numark Mixtrack Platinum FX controller, read-only djay beatgrid import and an unsigned Windows installer. The web version keeps working alongside it.

### Added
- **Desktop app** — a Tauri desktop app bundles the whole workspace, so it runs without a separate server. It shows a card-only splash while the library prepares, has a glyph-only icon, supports themed backgrounds, and shares the same interface as the web version. Native set creation works reliably. Audio analysis and provider lookups remain web-only in this preview.
- **Windows installer** — an unsigned Windows x64 installer is built on GitHub, bundling FFmpeg, ffprobe and Chromaprint. A fresh install starts with an empty library under the user's app data folder and ships no personal data.
- **Two-deck player and mixer** — two decks with pitch, bend, key lock, Sync, per-deck EQ and filter, trim and master level, and a djay-style mixer. Cue pads, playback gain with limiting, and smooth playhead, entrance and press-feedback motion round it out.
- **Numark Mixtrack Platinum FX support** — the controller drives the decks directly: jog scrubbing, Load, Cue, pad cues, pitch range with pickup, Sync, and browsing and loading from any list. Pressing play routes to a free deck. Tag banks, transition notes and loops are available from the pads, and the VU meters follow the audio. The buttons, pads, VU meters and jog screens light up, including a beat ring, and everything goes dark when the app quits. The deck layout fits the hardware. MIDI exists only in the desktop app.
- **Deck waveform panels** — each deck gets a scrolling waveform with a header, backed by a 3-band detail waveform. The panel sits at the top of the window by default, like djay, and a switch in Settings, «Θέση κυματομορφών decks», moves it back above the dock. The deck rows mirror around the mixer, the dock collapses, and the waveform colours follow the theme.
- **Beatgrid από djay** — an opt-in, read-only import brings djay Pro beatgrids into CrateMind. The djay library is read from a private copy and per-track analysis files are read in place, so djay files are never written to, moved or locked. A Settings card reports how many tracks matched and how many did not, and a button removes the imported grids again. Grids that djay draws straight are recognised by the typical beat rather than the worst few, so far more tracks are accepted as straight.
- **Downbeat tool** — a DOWNBEAT overlay on each deck's waveform (Set, ◀, ▶, ↺) lets you mark where bar 1 starts. The value lives only in CrateMind: it never moves djay beat positions, and removing or re-importing djay grids leaves it alone. Beat lines, estimate lines and bar numbers are drawn brighter, and grid lines stay readable on light waveform colours.
- **Shortcuts sheet** — an app-wide keyboard shortcuts sheet, and an updated psytrance substyle list with an info note for each option.

### Changed
- **Daily launch backups** — the database is snapshotted at most once every 24 hours after a light integrity check, and always before a pending schema upgrade. The last 3 backups are kept.
- **Consistent library metadata** — artist, title and tempo are shown from one consistent source across the Library, sets and recommendations, without ever rewriting source files. Manual identity decisions are preserved, and unchanged files are skipped on rescan.
- **Compact workspace** — the DJ workspace stays compact and ready for controller input, with the BPM and key badge under the pads in two-deck mode.
- **Greek djay UI** — the djay report and the downbeat button are in Greek, and the interface-language rule is tightened.
- **Retired the old Deno app** — the earlier Deno desktop app is gone, while the importer for its existing user databases stays.

### Fixed
- **Windows backups** — the backup snapshot is opened for writing before it is flushed to disk, and the self-test no longer leaves a catalog connection open on Windows.
- **djay track matching** — durations and bookmarked file paths are now read correctly, so more djay tracks match their CrateMind entries.
