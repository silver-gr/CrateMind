# Artist genre research

The Artists page's **Enrich** action researches musical genre through the configured server-side proxy. It uses Responses API `web_search`, rather than the old prompt-only DJ profile estimates. No extra package or search-provider account is required.

Configure `backend/.env`:

```dotenv
CRATEMIND_LLM1_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8317/v1
CRATEMIND_LLM1_API_KEY=<existing proxy key>
CRATEMIND_LLM1_MODEL=gpt-6-luna
CRATEMIND_LLM1_MODEL_EFFORT=max
```

The key stays on the backend. The native runtime uses its existing explicit per-device enrichment configuration instead of inheriting the checkout's `.env`.

## Workflow

1. Select artists with at least two present catalog tracks, excluding placeholders and human-confirmed entries. Previously generated estimates are eligible for this version of research.
2. Send one artist's name and up to three local track examples (title, artist credit, album, genre, label). Do not send local paths or audio.
3. Research primary sources such as artist sites and official label/Bandcamp pages. Use track examples to distinguish namesakes. MusicBrainz can help identify an artist, but missing or broad tags are not sufficient genre evidence.
4. Require a completed web-tool call, a completed structured response, the requested model/effort and evidence URLs present in the tool/citation output. This checks provenance of URLs; it does not independently prove every model interpretation of a page.
5. Stage a versioned finding in `ArtistReview.candidate_json`. Active genre/numeric fields remain intact. A failed artist remains retryable and completed findings survive later failures.
6. Show the proposed genre, Greek rationale and source links. Accepting a classified finding applies only genre-related fields and seals the artist. A manual outside-list correction remains possible and retains the research separately from the human choice.

**Allowed LLM labels:** Goa Trance, Prog-psy, Full-On, Morning Full-On, Night Full-On, Twilight, Darkpsy, Forest, Hi-Tech, Psycore, Zenonesque, Psydub.

The model returns `classified`, `outside_list` or `unresolved`. It must not force unrelated bass, ambient or other music into this list. Outside-list and unresolved findings cannot be accepted as an automatic genre; the user can review sources and choose manually.

Ready findings from this workflow are skipped by a repeat research click. Confirmed artists stay excluded even under forced research, and a confirmation made during an in-flight search wins over the arriving result. The older comparison endpoint remains compatible, but the page no longer presents a saved-versus-new comparison.

The reusable prompt, strict result schema and validation live in `backend/app/services/artist_genre_research.py`; the job orchestration is `backend/app/services/artist_intelligence.py`. No biographies, BPM/energy estimates, automatic file tagging or artist renaming are generated by this workflow.

## Luna result view

The shared web/Tauri Artists page displays only completed, versioned research whose model is exactly `gpt-6-luna`. Legacy model estimates and pending/error candidates are hidden. A rejected candidate is never resurfaced as a final result. Previously approved manual Luna research carries explicit provenance and retains its original human confirmation.

Each artist has one genre result, optional related genres, a confirmation action when needed, and collapsed rationale/source details. Old scene clusters, biographies and BPM/energy estimates are not part of this view. Corrections are revealed only on request. The Unknown Artist utility remains read-only and links to its tracks.
